## Selected work

### [Heavenly Weather](https://weather.heavenly.cl)

Chile-first aviation desk. Live METAR and TAF, a route GRAMET, GAMET-style bulletins, WRF and GFS point forecasts, South American synoptic charts, and a Cesium globe of Chilean aerodromes. Each product says whether the data is live, derived, or a fallback.

[weather.heavenly.cl](https://weather.heavenly.cl) · [source](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/weather)

### [KineVision](https://github.com/celestial-fix/kinevision)

Mobility coach for Bendita Hackathon 2025. MediaPipe reads the exercise in the browser, scores the movement, and answers by voice so both hands can stay on the work. Clinicians assign plans and review progress. Session video stays on the device unless the patient chooses to share it.

React, Vite, FastAPI, OpenCV. [source](https://github.com/celestial-fix/kinevision)

### [Libro de Mora](https://mora.oficinaslascondes.cl)

Late fees for a Chilean lease, including partial payments on different dates. The unpaid balance keeps accruing until it is covered. Pesos and UF, in the browser, with nothing stored on a server.

[mora.oficinaslascondes.cl](https://mora.oficinaslascondes.cl) · [source](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/mora)

### [Alongside](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/conmigo)

A video call beside the document being presented. Jitsi for the room, PDF.js for the pages, and the presenter's page follows everyone until they step off to read ahead. Static files. No account.

[source](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/conmigo)

### [Envía lite](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/envia_lite)

Personal mail merge: a template, a table, attachments, a full preview, then send. One Python file, a desktop app, or a container.

[source](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/envia_lite)

### [Valor UF](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/valoruf)

The day's UF from the Comisión para el Mercado Financiero, for agreements written in Chile's inflation-linked unit.

[source](https://github.com/heavenly-tech/valoruf)
